Questions, answered

What platforms do you specialize in managing?+

I have in-depth experience managing Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, and X (Twitter). Campaigns are tailored specifically to the tone, audience dynamics, and algorithm requirements of each platform.

How do you approach video editing for short-form content?+

Using CapCut and Adobe Premiere Pro, I build thumb-stopping hooks within the first 3 seconds, dynamic pacing, custom captions, motion graphics, and audio sync designed for virality and high viewer retention.

How do you measure and report campaign success?+

I track key performance indicators including engagement rate, reach, click-through rate, cost per acquisition, and ROAS using Meta Business Suite, Instagram Insights, and Google Analytics, providing transparent weekly or monthly reports.

Do you offer both project-based work and ongoing retainers?+

Yes. I offer end-to-end monthly social media management retainers, as well as project-based services like dedicated ad campaigns, video editing packages, or event photo/video coverage.
